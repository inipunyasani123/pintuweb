**A digital invitation is an event invitation in the form of a web page that you share as a link, usually over WhatsApp.** From one link, guests can read the event details, open the location map, save the date and confirm attendance (RSVP) — without installing any app.

## Features worth having

| Feature | Essential? | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Event name, date, time | Essential | The first thing guests look for |
| Location with a map button | Essential | Guests can start navigation straight away |
| Countdown | Recommended | Reminds guests without follow-up messages |
| Attendance confirmation (RSVP) | Recommended | Helps you plan catering and seating |
| Photo gallery | Optional | Adds atmosphere, especially for weddings |
| Background music | Optional | Make sure it can be muted and is properly licensed |
| Opening envelope or cover | Optional | Feels like opening a real invitation |
| Agenda and attendee registration | For corporate events | Conferences and gatherings need a session schedule |

## Protecting privacy

Invitations often include full names, family photos and home addresses. A few simple steps:

- **Include only what is needed.** The venue address is enough; there is no need for a home address if the event is held elsewhere.
- **Ask for the page to be kept out of search engines** (noindex), so the invitation can only be opened through the link you share.
- **Be careful with children's photos.** Consider images that do not clearly show faces or the home.
- **Remove or disable it after the event** if you do not want it to stay accessible.

## Tips for sharing on WhatsApp

- **Send personal messages, not just to groups.** A personal message feels more courteous, especially for elders and guests of honour.
- **Add a short introduction** before the link, such as a greeting and the event name, so it is not mistaken for spam.
- **Send a reminder** a day or two before the event, using the same link.
- **Check the link preview** (the title and image shown when the link is sent) before sharing widely.

## Digital or printed?

| Aspect | Digital invitation | Printed invitation |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per guest | Almost nothing once made | Rises with every copy |
| Delivery speed | Instant | Has to be delivered or posted |
| Changing details | Can be updated any time | Needs reprinting |
| RSVP | Straight from the page | By phone or message |
| Impression on elders | Depends on guests' habits | Often seen as more formal |

Many families use both: a digital invitation for most guests and a few printed invitations for elders or guests of honour.
