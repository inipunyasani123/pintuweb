Our mobile apps are built with React Native and Expo, so one codebase produces Android, iOS and web/PWA versions. The package covers UI design per screen, local data and offline mode where needed, plus notifications and an admin dashboard as required.

A web app has login and user roles, data management (add, edit, delete, search), kanban, calendar or report views, CSV/Excel export, and a responsive layout. If your staff all have phones and browsers, a web app is often enough and skips the app stores.

## What sets the price

1. **Screens and flows.** A five-screen cashier app differs hugely from a fifty-screen one.
2. **Online or local data.** Data kept on one phone is simpler. Data synced across devices needs a server and database.
3. **Logins and roles.** Owner, cashier and customer see different things; more roles mean more testing.
4. **Notifications and integrations.** Payments, receipt printers or third-party services each need testing.
5. **Design.** Per-screen branded UI takes longer than a template.

## Costs people forget

- **Developer accounts.** Publishing needs a developer account in your or your business's name. Fees and terms are set by [Google](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6112435) and [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/programs/), so check the official pages for current figures. We hand over files and source code; release is done with your account, following the [Expo guide](https://docs.expo.dev/distribution/app-stores/).
- **Server and database.** The Web App package includes hosting and a database for 1 year. Later costs are stated in the quote.
- **Updates.** Phone operating systems change every year. Extended maintenance can be requested monthly.
- **Transaction fees.** If you take payments, the payment provider charges its own fees.
