**Social media helps a small business reach people, while a website makes the business findable, trustworthy and independent of any one platform.** They do not replace each other. The better question is: when is social media alone no longer enough?

With 229 million internet users in Indonesia according to the 2025 APJII survey, almost every potential customer is already online. The difference is *how* they find you: through an algorithm-curated feed, through a marketplace search, or through a Google search.

## When social media alone is still enough

- The business is new and still testing products.
- Almost all customers come from your social circle or community.
- The product is simple, so customers rarely ask the same questions.

At this stage, a tidy [link-in-bio page](/en/services/link-in-bio) often helps: one link with WhatsApp, a menu and a catalogue.

## Signs your business needs a website

1. **Customers keep asking the same things** — price, location, how to order, opening hours. A website answers once for everyone.
2. **You want to appear when people search Google**, for example “catering in Trenggalek” or “air-con servicing”.
3. **Prospective clients need convincing**: proposals, tenders and B2B partnerships almost always start with checking your website.
4. **Sales depend too heavily on one platform.** A single algorithm or fee change can hit revenue immediately.
5. **You run ads** and need a fast destination page whose results you can measure.

## The most resilient strategy: use all three

Make the website your hub. Use social media to reach and engage, marketplaces to catch buyers who are ready to purchase, and the website as the home of all your official information — linked from your bio, shop description, business cards and even packaging.

That way, if one channel dips, customers still know where to find you.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a website replace Instagram?

No. Instagram remains important for reach and interaction. A website complements it as the place for complete information and an official address that can be found on Google.

### Will my own online store be quiet compared with a marketplace?

At first, visitors to your own store usually come from existing customers, social media and Google. That is why many sellers use both: marketplaces for new buyers, their own store for loyal customers and promotions.
