Tulungagung is a regency on the south coast of East Java, bordered by Kediri to the north, Blitar to the east and Trenggalek to the west. We are based here, so this guide maps the sectors you actually find in the regency.

## Why do Tulungagung businesses need a website?

Customers from outside the regency cannot see your premises before they visit or order, so they rely on what they can find online:

- **Visitors.** BPS counted 1,360,950 tourist visits to Tulungagung in 2025, and the regency's stretch of the southern coastal road (Jalur Lintas Selatan) was fully connected in mid-2026, opening up the beaches along the coast.
- **Craft buyers.** Tulungagung is known as the City of Marble. BPS records marble and onyx tiles, mosaics and basins from Tulungagung being exported to countries across the Americas, Europe and Asia.
- **Customers in nearby towns.** People in Kediri, Blitar or Trenggalek looking for a product or service can find you on their phone, provided your information is there.

Social media and marketplaces still help, but a website gives you one official address with everything: products, prices, location, opening hours and how to order.

## How do you reach buyers outside the regency and abroad?

- **State prices, or how to request one, clearly.** Distant buyers cannot haggle at the counter.
- **Explain shipping**: where you usually send, how you pack and how long it takes.
- **Add an English version** if you serve foreign tourists or importers.
- **Keep details identical** across the website, Business Profile, marketplaces and social media.
