## When is Simple enough?

If you get a dozen orders a day and are used to replying on WhatsApp, a tidy catalog already saves a lot of work: buyers see products, prices and photos, then tap an order button that sends their list to your chat. Many food sellers, craftspeople and small boutiques start here.

Signs you need Full: you answer the same question over and over ("how much is shipping?"), orders arrive after hours and get lost, or confirming payments takes hours.

## Why Full costs more

- **Payment gateway.** The integration must be tested across payment methods, statuses and cancellations.
- **Automatic shipping.** Courier rates depend on weight, origin and destination.
- **Admin dashboard.** Managing stock, order status and product data needs logins and access rights.
- **Testing.** A broken checkout means lost orders or money, so testing takes longer.
