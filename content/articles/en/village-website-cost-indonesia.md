## Three levels of village website

| Level | Content | PintuWeb package |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Profile and information | Village profile, government structure, local potential (tourism, small businesses, farming), news, gallery, map, contact | Standard Business, from {{from:standar-umkm}} |

| 3. Services and data | Letter requests, population data, complaints, budget transparency with logins | Custom Website or Web App |

Level 1 covers most villages: outsiders can find the village and its potential on Google, and residents know how to reach the village office. Standard Business includes 3–5 pages, a responsive design, domain, hosting and SSL, a contact form, Google Maps, basic SEO and 2 design revisions.

Level 3 is different in nature. Once a site stores residents' data, security, staff access rights and data responsibility come into play. That is why it is not a fixed package: we price it after a consultation.

## About the desa.id domain

A desa.id domain is reserved for village governments; it is not a domain anyone can buy. The Ministry of Communication and Digital Affairs (Komdigi) calls it a government digital asset and urges villages to manage it properly: renew before it expires, monitor its validity, keep the responsible person's details current and keep the website active.

Check these points with the authorities yourself (for example the district communications office or the [domain.go.id portal](https://domain.go.id)), because procedures change:

- who may apply and which documents are required,
- whether there is a fee and how much,
- who is the responsible person for the domain in the village government.

We do not promise to obtain government domains for you. What we do: prepare the site so it can be placed on a domain you already hold, or provide a commercial domain if the village does not yet have a desa.id one.

## What affects the cost

- **Pages and menus.** Extra pages are charged per page.
- **Service features.** A simple form costs far less than a system with village-staff logins.
- **Content readiness.** Tidy photos, profile text and local-potential data speed things up.
- **Who updates it.** Name a village operator and make news posting easy.
- **Support.** Our bug guarantee is {{guarantee}} days after launch; extended maintenance is optional.

When village officials change, note who holds the domain and hosting accounts. Village sites often go offline because a renewal date slipped through after a handover.

## What to ask a vendor

1. A written list of pages and features.
2. Who owns the domain, hosting and code after full payment.
3. How staff change passwords and post news.
4. The guarantee and the second-year cost.
