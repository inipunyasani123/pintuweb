**What a school website costs depends on its features, not on the school level: a profile site with news and a gallery is far cheaper than an online admissions system or a parent portal.** At PintuWeb a school profile site can use the Standard Business package from {{from:standar-umkm}}; heavier features are built as a Custom Website or Web App, priced to your needs.

One honest note first: we do not sell a package called "school website". We have general packages, and the job is matching what your school needs to the closest one.

The Standard Business package includes 3–5 pages (Home, About, Services, Blog, Contact), a responsive design, domain, hosting and SSL, a contact form, WhatsApp, Google Maps, basic SEO and 2 design revisions. For a school, the "Services" page usually becomes programs or majors and the "Blog" becomes school news.

## What moves the price

1. **Number of pages.** Extra pages are charged per page, so decide which ones you will really use.
2. **Features that store user data.** Anything that asks people to log in, upload files or keep student data adds build time, testing and responsibility.
3. **Content readiness.** Tidy photos, staff lists and profile text speed everything up.
4. **Who updates it.** If staff will post news regularly, ask how, and whether training is included.
5. **Support period.** Our bug guarantee is {{guarantee}} days after launch.

## Costs schools often forget

- **Domain and hosting renewal.** The first year is included; from the second year it is roughly {{renewal}} per year, and we remind you before it is due.
- **School-specific domains.** Extensions such as sch.id usually require document verification through the domain registry. Check the current rules with [PANDI](https://pandi.id) or an official registrar before deciding.
- **Someone to post content.** A site that is never updated feels abandoned. Name one person for news and announcements.
- **Photos of students.** Get parents' permission before showing children's photos.

## How to compare vendors

- Ask for a written list of pages and features, not only a total.
- Make sure the domain is registered to the school or foundation, not to the vendor.
- Ask whether code and hosting access are handed over after full payment. With PintuWeb, the domain, code and access are yours once you have paid.
- Open live examples. We have {{demos}} demos in the [demo gallery](/en/demos), including a school app for parents.
- Ask for the second-year cost and who helps when something breaks.
