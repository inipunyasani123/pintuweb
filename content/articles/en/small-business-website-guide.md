**Building a small business website comes down to seven steps: set the goal, choose the type of website, secure the domain, prepare the content, design and build, optimise for Google, and look after it once it is live.** You do not need to code to follow these steps; what matters most is a clear goal and ready content.

## Why small businesses need a website now

According to the 2025 survey by the Indonesian Internet Service Providers Association (APJII), Indonesia has 229,428,417 internet users, or 80.66% of the population. Meanwhile, the Ministry of MSMEs counts about 65.5 million micro, small and medium enterprises contributing 61.9% of GDP. Customers are already online — and so is the competition for their attention.

Social media helps you reach people, but a website gives you an official address that can be found on Google and that you fully control. We compare the two in [website vs social media](/en/articles/website-vs-social-media).

## Step 1 — Set one main goal

Write one sentence: “This website exists so that visitors …”. For example:

- … order products via WhatsApp.
- … trust us and ask for a quote.
- … find our location and opening hours.

This goal decides which pages you need and which button stands out most.

## Step 3 — Register the domain in your business's name

A domain is your website address, such as yourbusiness.com. Choose a name that is short, easy to spell and matches your business name. For Indonesian domains, PANDI (the .id registry) allocates .co.id to registered businesses (requiring a business identification number, NIB, and an ID card) and .biz.id to micro, small and medium enterprises. More in our [domain and hosting guide](/en/articles/domain-and-hosting-basics).

Most importantly: **make sure the domain is registered in your name**, not the web developer's.

## Step 4 — Prepare the core content

Content is the most common cause of delays. Prepare:

- A 3–5 sentence business description: who you are, what you sell and for whom.
- A list of products or services, with prices if you want to show them.
- Real photos: products, premises, team. Bright phone photos are good enough.
- Contact details: WhatsApp number, email, address, opening hours.
- Proof: genuine customer testimonials, certificates or press coverage, if any.

If anything is missing, we can write copy from a short brief.

## Step 5 — Design and build

Good design for a small business is not the busiest one, but the one that helps visitors understand quickly and act easily:

- Design for phones first, since most visitors arrive on one.
- One clear main button, such as “Order on WhatsApp”.
- Fast pages; Google recommends the main content appears within 2.5 seconds.

At PintuWeb, you approve the design before the build starts, and you can open the preview yourself throughout.

## Step 6 — Optimise for Google

A new website needs help to be found: clear page titles and descriptions, a sitemap, HTTPS, a mobile-friendly layout, and registration in Google Search Console. The full list is in our [new website SEO checklist](/en/articles/new-website-seo-checklist).

If you have a physical location, also create a Google Business Profile so you appear on Google Maps and in local search.

## Step 7 — Look after it after launch

A website that is never updated slowly falls behind. Schedule simple updates: new prices and products, fresh photos, an occasional article, and checking that forms and the WhatsApp button still work. ## Frequently asked questions

### Can a small business build its own website?

Yes, with a website builder. The trade-off is that you handle the design, writing and SEO settings yourself, and design and feature options are limited. A web developer saves time and usually delivers a faster, tidier website.

### How long until a website appears on Google?

A new website usually starts being indexed within 1–2 weeks of submitting it to Google Search Console. Rankings grow gradually with helpful content.

### What should I prepare before contacting a web developer?

Your goal, business name, a short description, a list of products or services, photos and contact details. Examples of websites you like also help a lot.
