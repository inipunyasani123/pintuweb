## Six factors that decide the price

1. **Number of pages.** One promotional page is far quicker to build than a five-page website with about, services, blog and contact pages.
2. **Features.** A simple form and a WhatsApp button are inexpensive. A shopping cart, online payments, automatic shipping rates, a booking system or a member area add development and testing time.
3. **How custom the design is.** A design built from scratch around your brand takes longer than changing the colours of a template.
4. **Content.** If your copy and photos are ready, work goes faster. If they need writing or shooting, that is extra work.
5. **Integrations.** Connecting the website to other systems — a payment gateway, a calendar or a point-of-sale app — means checking documentation and testing.
6. **Support after launch.** The length of maintenance and the scope of the guarantee also affect the price.

## Cheap or expensive: what to check

A lower price is not always cheaper in the end. Before paying a deposit, ask:

- **Whose name will the domain be in?** It should belong to you or your business.
- **Are the source code and hosting access handed over?** Without them, you depend on one provider forever.
- **What does year two cost?** A cheap first year is sometimes followed by expensive renewals.
- **What does the guarantee cover?** Get it in writing: how many days, and what is included.
- **Can you open examples that are already live?** Live examples are more trustworthy than mock-up images. We publish {{demos}} demos in our [demo gallery](/en/demos).

## How to save without cutting corners

- **Start with what you need most.** Many businesses begin with a [landing page](/en/services/landing-page) and grow it into a full website later; the code and design can be reused.
- **Prepare your own content.** A business description, product list and real photos shorten the work and usually convince customers better.
- **Postpone features that are not urgent.** A shop with few orders can start with a catalogue and WhatsApp ordering, then move to automatic payments as orders grow.
- **Ask for a custom quote.** For large projects or several websites at once, the price can be discussed.
